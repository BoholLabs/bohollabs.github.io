Subscription terms

Effective September 11, 2026

These terms describe the StockLite Pro subscription offered by Bohol Labs through Google Play. Review the plan and payment details before confirming your purchase.

Your subscription at a glance

Choose monthly or yearly billing. Pro includes custom date reports, CSV and PDF report exports, and an ad-free app. You can manage renewal in Google Play.

1. What Pro includes

An active Pro subscription unlocks custom date ranges for sales reports, CSV and PDF sales report exports, and removes ads throughout StockLite. Inventory, checkout, daily, weekly and monthly reports, and local backups remain available without a subscription.

2. Plans and pricing

StockLite Pro offers monthly and yearly auto-renewing plans. The Philippine launch prices are ₱99 per month or ₱999 per year, billed as a single payment for the selected period. Prices and availability can vary by country, currency and applicable taxes. The amount, billing period and any offer shown in Google Play at checkout apply to your purchase.

No free trial is included unless one is explicitly displayed at checkout with its duration and subsequent price. Future pricing changes will follow Google Play’s notice and consent requirements.

3. Payment and renewal

Google Play processes your payment using your Google account. Your subscription renews automatically for the selected billing period unless you cancel before renewal. Check Google Play for your next billing date and payment details.

4. Managing or cancelling

Open Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → StockLite Pro to manage or cancel. You can also visit Google Play subscriptions.

After cancellation, Pro normally remains available through the paid period. Uninstalling StockLite does not cancel the subscription. See Google Play’s cancellation guidance.

5. Refunds

Cancelling renewal does not automatically request a refund. Refund eligibility depends on Google Play’s policies and applicable consumer law. Use Google Play’s refund guidance or contact Bohol Labs for help with a purchase issue. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under applicable law.

6. Restoring purchases and access

Use Restore purchases in StockLite while signed into the same Google Play account used to subscribe. An internet connection may be required to purchase, restore or verify your subscription. Google Play and RevenueCat help verify whether Pro is active. Pending, expired or refunded purchases may not provide Pro access.

Restoring a subscription restores purchase access; it does not restore inventory or sales records from another device.

7. Your records when Pro ends

When Pro expires, custom date reports and report exports become unavailable, and ads may appear again. Expiration itself does not delete saved inventory, sales records or backups. Free features remain available. Keep your own backups: uninstalling the app, clearing its data, or losing your device can remove locally stored records.

8. Privacy and support

Read our Privacy Policy for information about data handling and service providers. For subscription or purchase support, contact bohollabs@gmail.com. Include a description of the issue and, if relevant, your Google Play order reference. Do not send passwords or full payment-card details.

9. Updates to these terms

We may update these terms as StockLite or its subscription service changes. Updated terms will show a new effective date on this page. Material changes will be communicated through the app or relevant store notices as appropriate and as required by law.
