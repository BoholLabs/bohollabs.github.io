StockLite: Inventory & Sales · Android

Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: September 9, 2026

This policy explains how Bohol Labs handles information when you use StockLite, including its inventory tools, device backups, advertising, and optional Pro subscriptions.

Your store records stay in your control.

StockLite works without a StockLite account. Inventory and sales records are stored on your device. Advertising and subscription services, when enabled, process certain information as described below.

1. Inventory, sales, and photos

StockLite stores product names and variants, prices, stock quantities, movements, sale line items, discounts, payment-method labels, amounts tendered, change, timestamps, and app preferences on your device. Photos you choose and preset icons are copied into app storage to display products and include them in backups.

We do not operate a StockLite cloud inventory database. The app does not send your store records or product photos to Bohol Labs, AdMob, or RevenueCat. Recording a sale as “GCash” or “Cash” only records the payment method; StockLite does not process that customer payment or access your GCash account.

The app accesses images you select through your device’s image picker and files you choose for backup import or export. Its inventory features do not request contacts, microphone access, or precise GPS location.

2. Backups and device storage

Backups contain inventory, sales, stock movements, and product images. Eligible automatic backups run while the app is open or when you return to it. Up to seven daily copies are retained; older daily copies are removed. Manual, imported, and recovery backups remain until deleted or the app’s data is removed.

Exported backups go to a location you choose. If you select cloud storage or share a file, that provider or recipient receives its contents under their own terms. StockLite does not automatically upload exports. Android or your device manufacturer may separately back up or transfer app data according to your device settings.

Backup files are not password-protected or encrypted by StockLite. Store and share them carefully. A backup on the same device does not protect against loss of that device.

3. Advertising

When advertising is enabled, the free version uses Google AdMob banners on Dashboard and Reports. Banners are hidden for users with active Pro access. We request non-personalized ads; this does not mean the advertising service collects no data.

Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), advertising IDs and other device or account identifiers, app/ad interactions, and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google’s consent service may process consent choices.

Where applicable, review consent through “Ad privacy choices” in Settings. You can also manage your advertising ID in Android settings. See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Pro subscriptions

When subscriptions are enabled, Google Play Billing and RevenueCat check Pro access and purchase status and restore purchases. RevenueCat assigns a random app-user identifier and processes purchase history, product and transaction information, subscription status, and relevant platform information such as locale and currency. Status may be checked at app startup or resume, even without a new purchase.

This information supports paid features, purchase validation, fraud prevention, and subscription analytics. Google Play handles payment credentials; StockLite does not receive full payment-card details. We do not supply your inventory database or product photos to RevenueCat. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

5. Support and this website

If you email us, we receive your address and the information you include. We use it to answer questions, investigate issues, and handle privacy requests. Avoid sending store backups or sensitive information unless needed for your request.

Bohol Labs has added no advertising, analytics scripts, or contact form to this policy page. Hosting providers may process ordinary connection information, including IP addresses, requested URLs, and browser information, to deliver and secure this website.

6. Sharing, security, and international processing

The providers described above handle information for their services. We may also disclose information we hold when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, or prevent fraud. We do not sell your inventory records or product photos.

Local records rely on Android’s app storage protections and your device security. Google’s advertising SDK and RevenueCat use encrypted connections to their services. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Providers may process information outside your country under their privacy terms and applicable safeguards.

7. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local records remain until removed through available app controls or by clearing StockLite’s app storage in Android settings. Clearing storage removes local records and in-app backups. Deleting a product does not erase historical sales, existing backups, or all previously saved images. Use Android’s clear-storage control to remove all local app data, and separately delete exported files and device/cloud backup copies you control.

Clearing data or uninstalling does not cancel a Google Play subscription or necessarily delete records held by Google or RevenueCat. Manage subscriptions in Google Play. Providers may retain purchase records for subscription operation, fraud prevention, accounting, or legal obligations under their policies.

We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle requests, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal obligations. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of personal information. Email us to make a request. We may need information to verify and locate records. We cannot remotely access or delete records that exist only on your device.

8. Children

StockLite is designed for store owners and people managing business inventory. It is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information to us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when features, providers, or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated through this page and, where required, an appropriate in-app notice or consent request.

10. Contact Bohol Labs

For privacy questions or requests concerning StockLite, email bohollabs@gmail.com.
